BV Icon Collection

Obsidian, Precision, Aether, Gilded, Runebound, Fel, BV Signature and Minimal
and Icon Echo, including their geometric variants and alpha masks, are original BV designs. BV Signature uses the steel/cyan visual language of
the approved BV logo; the logo master remains unchanged.
Their editable source is tools/build_icon_skins.py in the BV UI project.
No Masque skin artwork or third-party button skin files are included.
These assets are part of BV Addon Suite and use the project's distribution terms.

The development-only HTML preview references the Blizzard Immolate icon remotely.
That icon and Masque itself are not bundled in this collection.
