BV Addon Suite - Map Data

NPCs, trainers, services, instance entrances and objects for BV Addon Suite.

their own license; they read this data when the package is installed and
work without it.

Source
  vMaNGOS world database, https://github.com/vmangos/core (GPL-2.0),
  release "db_latest" (SQLite database), tables creature,
  creature_template, faction_template, game_event_creature, gameobject,
  gameobject_template, areatrigger_teleport, areatrigger_template,
  npc_vendor, item_template, quest_template, creature_questrelation,
  creature_involvedrelation, taxi_nodes and transports.

What was derived and changed
  Creatures of patch 1.12 on the two continents (holiday event spawns and
  technical creatures such as triggers left out) with their name, subtitle,
  service flags, level range, rank, trainer kind and whether they are
  hostile to the Alliance or the Horde; spawns of one creature close to
  each other were merged into one point with their number. Mailboxes,
  meeting stones (with their level range), anvils and forges with their
  positions. Instance entrances with the instance's name, required level
  and map ID. What vendors sell (item name and ID); the quests NPCs give
  and take (title, level, races). Standing NPCs' heights. Flight masters
  with their faction. Zeppelins, ships and the Deeprun Tram as pairs of
  stops with the time of a round trip. World positions were converted to coordinates (0..1) of the
  continent maps of the client's UiMapAssignment table. Nothing else of the
  database is included. Generated by tools/build_map_data.py of the
  BV Addon Suite project (2026-10-10).
