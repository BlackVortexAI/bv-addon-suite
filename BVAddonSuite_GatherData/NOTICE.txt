BV Addon Suite - Gather Data

Known herb and ore spawn points for BV Gather.

their own license; BV Gather reads this data when the package is installed.

Source
  vMaNGOS world database, https://github.com/vmangos/core (GPL-2.0),
  release "db_latest" (SQLite database), tables gameobject and
  gameobject_template.

What was derived and changed
  Gameobjects of type 3 whose names are herbs or ore deposits were taken
  with their world positions. Each position was assigned to its zone (by
  the area ID of the terrain chunk under it, else by the zone rectangles of
  the client's UiMapAssignment table) and converted to map coordinates of
  that zone (0..1). Only coordinates and names are kept; nothing else of the
  database is included. Generated by tools/build_known_spawns.py of the
  BV Addon Suite project (2026-10-09).

Written offer for the corresponding source
  The data is generated from the database named above with the generator
  named above. On request we provide the generator and the exact inputs
  used for this version.
